# Routines and goals

For daily users who want to track habits and longer outcomes without turning them into overdue tasks. Orbit keeps three kinds of "repeat" apart: recurring tasks, Routines and Goals.

## Before you start

- At least one Area to own the Routine or Goal (the Inbox works until you decide).
- Know which one you need:

  | | Recurring task | Routine | Goal |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Example | Pay rent monthly | Medicine daily for 14 days | Read 5 books |
  | If you miss it | stays overdue until done | that day is missed, nothing piles up | progress just does not move |
  | Tracks | next due date | check-ins, streak, completion rate | percent toward a target |

## Steps

1. Create a Routine on the Routines screen (**New routine**). One sheet sets the name, schedule (daily, chosen weekdays, every N days, or N times a week), length (ongoing, or a number of days from a start date), time of day and an optional reminder. You can also capture one in a single line with the quick-add parser.
2. Check in from Home or the Routines screen: one tap marks the day done. A skipped day does not break a streak; a missed one does. A time-boxed Routine archives itself at its end date.
3. Create a Goal on the Goals screen (**New goal**): pick an Area and how progress is measured. Count tracks done linked items out of all linked, Target is a number you reach with progress entries, Manual is done or not. Add a target date to see whether you are **behind pace**.
4. Add Milestones (dated checkpoints inside one Goal) and sub-goals (one level deep) from the Goal's card. A Routine can optionally count toward a Goal.

## Verify it worked

Home shows today's due Routines and each Goal's progress. Checking a Routine in raises its streak, and the same check-in appears on another device after sync (and in the Android Routines widget).

## Troubleshooting

**A Routine does not show as due today.** Its schedule or start date does not include today, or it is archived. Open it from the Routines screen to check.

**A day I forgot is not "overdue".** By design: it is missed, not overdue. Use a recurring task if you need it to stay overdue until done.

**Reminders are late on Android.** See [Android app, Troubleshooting](android.md#troubleshooting).
